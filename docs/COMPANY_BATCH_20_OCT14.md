## Compensation and off-campus eligibility

Employer names are a shortlist signal, not a salary filter. None of the feed checks establishes the salary of a specific job or guarantees that a college student can apply. ₹8 LPA+ CTC can include variable pay or equity; internship stipends are a different measure. Campus results do not establish the pay or eligibility of an off-campus posting.

## Implementation

- `config.py` adds twelve Workday configurations, four Greenhouse boards, Coupa on Lever, Netradyne on SmartRecruiters and Amagi on Workable. `netapp.py` is registered separately.
- New Greenhouse boards require confirmed India locations. InMobi city-only labels are normalized only for known Indian cities observed in the employer feed. Coupa requires Lever country code IN.
- Workday normally discovers the live India facet. BrowserStack instead scans its complete global board and verifies India from each detail response because its visible India-location facet omits Mumbai office labels. Missing country details become partial failures.
- F5 IDs come from the second bullet field; the first is a non-unique zero. Counts and deduplication use real requisition IDs.
- NetApp follows actual TalentBrew next-page links, checks page/total/unique IDs and fetches complete `.ats-description` text.
- Workable failure is distinguishable from valid empty output; full descriptions are retained and Amagi uses explicit India country metadata.
- Subsequent NetApp, Workable and Workday India collections refresh saved description-derived fields while preserving first-seen dates and dismissals.
- Original 75-company manifest and October 8 batch audit keep their original scopes.
